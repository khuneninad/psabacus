A Journey to Excellence in mental arithmetic, brain development, and foundational learning.

PS ABACUS (under Piyushlok Education) was founded with a singular vision: to liberate children from math anxiety and inspire genuine joy in learning through scientific, sensory-motor mental arithmetic techniques — A Journey to Excellence.

Our Guiding Principles

What sets PS ABACUS apart in modern educational pedagogy.

Whole Brain Growth

Traditional school maths activates only the analytical left brain. Abacus bead visualization engages both hemispheres for photographic memory and spatial agility.

Original Curriculum

No generic third-party workbooks. Every exercise in our curriculum is classroom-tested and graded sequentially for optimum cognitive retention.

Individual Care

Every child's learning velocity is honored. Our small batch sizes guarantee that no child is left behind or feels overwhelmed.
