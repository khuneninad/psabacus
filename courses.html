Which Course Is Right For Your Child?

Each PS ABACUS program targets distinct neurological and developmental milestones. Many students enroll concurrently in Abacus + Handwriting or Vedic Maths + Handwriting.

Abacus (Ages 5-14)

Best for young children developing foundational number sense. Overcomes math phobia and instills lifelong lightning-fast mental visualization skills.

Vedic Maths (Ages 9+)

Best for middle & high schoolers, Olympiad aspirants, and competitive exam preparation. Provides instantaneous mental calculation shortcuts.

Handwriting (All Ages)

Essential for students losing marks due to messy handwriting or slow speed during board and term examinations. Fast visible improvement.
